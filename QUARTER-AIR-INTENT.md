# Quarter-air intent check

`src/physics/simulation.ts` now reads the sideways share of travel at a quarter lip. Deck carry remains unchanged below about 1.7° off-axis, fades through about 4.6°, then becomes a small inward return. This applies to natural quarter rollout. A held LS side input above 45% can steer a quarter air inward during the rise and near the apex; the correction is capped at 9 m/s² and -2.25 m/s along the ramp axis. It stops once vertical speed falls below -0.5 m/s, so late descent cannot pull a rider back through coping. Straight LS-forward deck exits retain their prior launch calculation.

`node tests/quarter-air-intent.browser.mjs` passed 12/12 cases on the production front and back wood quarters at 15 m/s: straight neutral airs returned to transition; straight LS-forward exits landed on deck; 5° and 12° LS-forward approaches returned to transition; side input begun 0.2 s after takeoff returned a straight deck-bound launch to transition. A combined body-flip and turn input also reached the transition side on both quarters. That last case asserts trajectory and actual flip/turn activation, not trick grade. `node tests/early-quarter-pop.browser.mjs` exited 0.
