contracts. It does not identify the actual repository's bug. The matrix has 47

RETAIN THESE EXPLICIT PRODUCT REQUIREMENTS
Mounted RS is tricks/preload/manuals, even while stationary. On-foot RS is camera
unless UI owns it. Keep Arcade/Pro, Regular/Goofy and current D-pad functions.

A recognized Pro RS Bri/Inward gesture can use the shared direct-takeoff transaction;
it must not require a separate manual bunny hop. Preserve accepted charge through
the scoop. Do not truncate a low attempt into an under-foot catch. Physically blocked
or expired input can be rejected with a reason; an eligible accepted attempt is not
silently refused merely to guarantee a landing. Uncharged is not automatically failure
when the ramp already supplies adequate air.

LT+RT with LS up/down/diagonals can begin bounded body-flip/spin control after takeoff,
including during Inward/Bri. That is deliberate gameplay angular authority, not a
second linear jump and not a claim of passive biomechanical accuracy.

Preserve gentle-RS manual catches and grind lean/RS exits. A current single-wheel
landing intent must not be overwritten by a universal two-wheel assist. Preserve the
existing combo-banking policy and exactly-once outcomes.

NON-NEGOTIABLES
One motion authority. One launch transaction. One confirmed result event.
Charge, locomotion, body action and equipment phase can coexist.
A 180 body turn does not reverse translational velocity.
Ordinary ramp-following rotation is not a scored flip.
Endpoint quaternions alone cannot distinguish zero/one/two complete revolutions.
Read impact velocity BEFORE resolution, at the actual moving contact point.
A safe endpoint does not make an obstructed capture/re-entry path safe.
Linear fixed-orientation shape queries are not automatically rotational sweeps.
No collision immunity, invisible landing planes, teleport-to-catch, hidden airtime,
forced single-flip stop, or per-obstacle secret boost.
