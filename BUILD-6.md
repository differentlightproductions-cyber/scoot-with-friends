# Scoot with Friends update

The title changed; Lazer remains the scooter brand and existing saved part IDs remain compatible.

- Restored the small box to its original center lane and lengthened its landing. Only the spine and large transfer exchange outer sides.
- Added a small bench on the spine-facing edge of the small box. All park benches now have solid rideable seats and two grindable coping edges. On foot, B sits/stands near a bench; A or Y can also leave the seat.
- Scooter builder outlines and lights the selected part and smoothly focuses the camera. RS rotates/zooms, LB+RS pans; mouse drag rotates, Shift/right-drag pans, and the wheel zooms.
- Bri animation now computes an absolute shoulder-relative transform each frame, avoiding accumulated sideways drift and accidental rider lift. Bri rotates backward with bar turn; inward rotates forward with less bar turn. Both hands remain anchored through the motion.
- Fingerwhip reach stays forward/outside the body. Rider faces have eyes, brows, noses, ears and mouths; hands have palms and segmented fingers. Pants include waist/pocket details and knee joints. Thigh anchors follow the actual hips through bending rather than a separate fixed position.

Checks include the existing riding/controller and customization suites, both directions through all center lanes, bench sitting/standing/landing/hopping, 300 bri animation frames, bent hip attachment, and editor focus/pan/zoom. The production build succeeds. Physical controller feel remains subject to hands-on feedback.

The public game and the standalone Windows release remain usable independently of this coding session. The previous portable ZIP is preserved when updating the current one.
